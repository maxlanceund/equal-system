## Abstract

The "survival coercion" that underlies the old system—marriage, childbearing, and breadwinning—the mechanism by which non-compliance leads to material abandonment—is being dismantled by changes in technology, economy, and cognitive structures. The intensity of pressure to marry and bear children has reached a historical peak—not a revival of the old system, but the release of residual signals before the boundary: the final manifestation before zeroing.

Based on verifiable data, this paper argues that the old system's coercion is being released, and meaning is turning in four directions: from "task completion" to "closed-loop construction," from "external validation" to "internal verification," from "survival exchange" to "free choice," and from "biological contingency" to "technological design." The reproductive revolution is the first threshold on the path to becoming god.

**Keywords**: survival coercion, release of meaning, boundary acceleration, principle of finite interval, becoming god

## 1. Introduction — A Disappearing Interval


### 1.1 The Problem

There is a phenomenon worth questioning:

**The voice urging marriage and childbearing grows louder, while marriage and fertility rates grow lower.**

If a system is still effective, it does not need to be repeatedly urged. If it is still effective, its execution rate does not continue to decline. When a building begins to creak loudly, it is not getting stronger—it is about to collapse.

In 2026, fertility rates in all major countries have fallen below the replacement level. South Korea: 0.80. Japan: 1.14. China: 1.09. Women's participation in higher education and research continues to rise. The decline in marriage and fertility is synchronous with the release of women's cognitive capabilities.

But in the same period, the intensity of discourse urging marriage and childbearing has reached a historical peak. Execution rate declines; urging intensity rises. This inverse pair is itself a signal: the old system's coercion is fading—it is releasing residual signals before the boundary, trying to sustain itself.

This paper answers three questions:

**What is happening to the old system? Why is it happening? Where is this ultimately taking us?**

The core proposition is that the underlying mechanism of the old system—marriage, childbearing, breadwinning—is "survival coercion": non-compliance leads to material abandonment. This mechanism is being dismantled by technology. After coercion disappears, meaning will turn in four directions. The reproductive revolution is the first threshold on the path to becoming god.

> ¹ Ulrich Beck, in *Risk Society* (1986), proposed the theory of "individualization": traditional institutions (marriage, family) are losing their social integrative functions, and individuals are forced to bear the responsibility of life decisions themselves.


### 1.2 Positioning of the Paper

This paper is a combined application of *The Limit Theory of Meaning* (Lanceund, 2026a), *Boundary Acceleration* (Lanceund, 2026b), and *The Principle of Finite Interval* (Lanceund, 2026c).

The relationships among these three papers are progressive:
- *The Limit Theory of Meaning* defines the structure of the boundary: meaning is defined only within a finite interval; beyond the interval, it is undefined.
- *The Principle of Finite Interval* describes the internal structure of the interval: all finite intervals share four characteristics.
- *Boundary Acceleration* describes the dynamic behavior of a system before the boundary: signal density rises before the boundary, then returns to zero.

This paper applies these three frameworks to the specific social system of "marriage—childbearing—breadwinning."

The boundary-marking work of this paper shares the same structure as Wittgenstein's work in the *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus*. Wittgenstein drew a boundary for the expression of thought—what can be said must be said clearly, and what cannot be said must be passed over in silence. This paper draws a boundary for meaning: meaning is defined only within the finite experiential interval of a finite system; beyond the interval it is undefined. Both refuse to extend meaning beyond the boundary.

The endpoint of this paper points to "becoming god"—the process by which humanity transitions from being governed by natural laws to actively designing its own mode of existence. The reproductive revolution is the first threshold on this path.


### 1.3 Explanation of the Three Core Concepts

Before proceeding, a few concrete examples illustrate the three core frameworks on which this paper relies. These examples carry no argumentative weight; they only aid comprehension.

**The Limit Theory of Meaning: a speech**

You stand on stage giving a speech. You speak, the audience reacts, you adjust your rhythm or content according to the reactions—this is a closed loop. The feedback you receive is within the interval; you have the opportunity to respond; meaning arises in this cycle.

After the speech ends, you record a video. The video is watched, liked, and commented on by many—but these feedback events occur after you have left the stage. You are no longer in that interval. You can read the comments afterward, but that is no longer the closed loop of "speaking" but a different closed loop of "reading comments."

The point of the Limit Theory of Meaning is: meaning is defined only within the interval in which you can receive and respond. Beyond that interval, meaning is undefined—not "meaningless," but "undefinable."

**Boundary Acceleration: an exam**

You have a two-hour exam. You spend the first 100 minutes answering questions. In the last 20 minutes, you realize you still have three big questions left. You do not maintain your previous pace—you accelerate. Your handwriting becomes messier, calculations faster; you stop reading the questions carefully and start writing directly.

The signal density in the last 20 minutes is higher than in the preceding 100 minutes. This is boundary acceleration: in the period before the boundary, signal density is higher than average.

If you finish early, you can submit your paper smoothly. But if there is residual signal before the boundary, signal density must rise—because signals must be released before the boundary.

**The Principle of Finite Interval: a time-limited shopping trip**

You have only 10 minutes to buy everything you need in a supermarket. When you enter, the paths you can take, the items you can get, the time you can use—all are given (input already filtered). Your attention competes among various goods, determining what you eventually buy and what you leave (signals competing in finite time). As time runs out, you speed up and hesitate less (acceleration before the boundary). In the end, your options are limited to what remains on the shelves, not everything (options already constrained).

This is the Principle of Finite Interval: regardless of content, as long as you face a finite interval, this four-step structure appears—filtered input, signal competition, boundary acceleration, and constrained options.

These examples serve only to explain concepts, not as argument. The argument is carried by the data and logical analysis in the following sections.

## 2. The Old System as a Finite Interval


### 2.1 The Limit Theory of Meaning: Meaning Is Defined Only Within the Interval

The central thesis of *The Limit Theory of Meaning* (Lanceund, 2026a) is that meaning is a signal produced by a finite system within a finite interval. Outside the interval, meaning is undefined—not "zero," but "no longer defined."

This framework has two corollaries:

1. Meaning must be accomplished through a closed-loop operation: action → receive feedback → adjust direction. This process must be completed within the interval.

2. The validation button must be inside the interval. Placing the validation button outside the interval (posthumous recognition, historical judgment, an afterlife) is a structural error—because you will never receive a response.


### 2.2 The Old System Is a Finite Interval

Following the four-step structure of *The Principle of Finite Interval* (Lanceund, 2026c), the old system takes the following form:

**Input already filtered**: At birth, an individual's class, region, era, and family conditions are already determined. The pool of potential marriage partners has already been pre-filtered by variables such as geography, culture, class, ethnicity, and education. No one can randomly encounter any person on earth.

**Signals competing in finite time**: The individual's life span is finite; the fertility window is finite; energy is finite. Within this limited window, different options (marry/not marry, early/late childbearing, one child/multiple children, whom to marry) compete for the individual's time and resources. Social discourse, family pressure, and peer examples all send signals to you simultaneously.

**Acceleration before the boundary**: As age approaches certain thresholds, social pressure, family urging, and personal anxiety all rise simultaneously. This process is not gradual but accelerating. The closer to the boundary, the higher the signal density.

**Options already constrained**: In the old system, "not marrying" and "not having children" are not available choices; they are "failure options." You can choose whom to marry and when, but you cannot choose "not marrying and still living normally." The options are given within the old system's boundaries, not fully open.


### 2.3 The Nature of the Old System: A Coercive Closed Loop

The logic of the old system can be described as a closed loop:

> You are born → you are educated to marry → you marry → you bear children → you support the family → you grow old → you die.

This is the "only legitimate path" in the old system. Those who deviate are marked as "failures"—materially insecure, socially marginalized, and subjected to persistent pressure over time.

This loop is effective not because it is "right," but because it was the only viable survival path at the time. When technology, economy, and cognitive structures change and provide other viable paths, this loop will lose its coercive force.


### 2.4 Determining the Current Phase of the Old System

According to finite interval theory, a system approaching its boundary will exhibit the following three observable characteristics. These three indicators constitute verifiable signals that the old system is approaching its boundary, and the subsequent sections will expand on this argument in turn.

**Indicator 1: Decline in system internal execution rate.**

The core directives of the old system (marriage, childbearing) are being executed by fewer and fewer individuals. Declining marriage rates and fertility rates mean the "population density" within the interval is decreasing. This is a signal that the system is approaching its boundary—when directive execution continuously declines, the system can no longer drive individuals into its orbit as it once did.

**Indicator 2: Emergence of alternative options outside the system boundary.**

When women can survive independently, institutions can provide for old age, and technology can replace reproductive functions, complete alternative survival paths have appeared outside the old system. A system's coercive force depends on "having no other choice"—when choices appear, the system's coercive force begins to decay. The existence of alternatives is itself proof that the old system's boundary has been breached.

**Indicator 3: The system itself emits signals of abnormal intensity.**

The historically peak intensity of pressure to marry and bear children is the old system, as a system, releasing signals before the boundary. When a system's signal output is inversely related to its execution rate (execution declining, but urging intensity rising), it indicates that the system is entering the boundary acceleration phase. It is not "getting stronger," but "clearing inventory."

### 3.1 Definition of Boundary Acceleration

*Boundary Acceleration* (Lanceund, 2026b) argues that if a system still holds unreleased signals before reaching its boundary, signal density will first rise sharply and then return to zero. Geometrically, it is a spike; mathematically, it is the logical necessity of "residual signals must be released within a finite window."

If a system has no residual signals at the boundary, the boundary may be smooth. But if the system still has unreleased signals before the boundary and those signals cannot be released after the boundary, then signal density must rise before the boundary—otherwise, the signals cannot be fully released before the boundary.

This means: **signal density rising is not the system getting stronger, but clearing inventory.**

### 3.3 Execution Rate Is Declining

In 2025, China recorded 6.763 million marriage registrations—roughly half of the 13.47 million peak in 2013 (Table 1). Births in 2025 totaled 7.92 million, with a birth rate of 5.63‰, the lowest since 1949. The total fertility rate was about 1.09, far below the replacement level of 2.1. The unmarried rate for men aged 30–34 was 26.8%, and for women aged 30–34, 12.1%. The average number of children ever born to women aged 18–44 was 1.13.

These data point to the same fact: fewer people are actually entering the old system. This is not a short-term fluctuation, but a long-term structural decline.


### 3.4 Urging Intensity Is Rising

Over the same period, the density of discourse urging marriage and childbearing, the frequency of family gatherings that pressure marriage and childbearing, and the heat of public discussion on bride price and marriage/childbearing—all stand at historical highs.

These two sets of data form a contrast structure: **execution rate declines while urging intensity rises.**

This contrast itself is evidence of boundary acceleration—the old system is not sustained by "more people still executing," but by "releasing maximum signals before the boundary." It is clearing inventory, not expanding orders.

Why does declining execution rate lead to rising signals? The old system is not a passively disappearing structure, but one that actively releases signals before the boundary. When a system's coercive force derives mainly from "everyone believes in it," as soon as "everyone believes" begins to loosen, those who have tied their own meaning to the system will urge others with greater intensity to return to the interval. Their behavior is not "caring for others," but "maintaining their own existential legitimacy"—because the emptier the system, the more those who remain inside appear to have made a mistake.

This is precisely the "last manifestation before zeroing" described in *Boundary Acceleration*—the historically peak intensity of pressure to marry and bear children is the old system entering the boundary acceleration phase. The spike itself is proof that zeroing is imminent.

## 4. The Release of Survival Coercion — How Technology Is Dismantling the Old System's Pillars


### 4.1 Definition of Survival Coercion

Survival coercion has two levels.

**Physical survival coercion**: hunger, disease, cold, physical injury. If unmet, the body dies. This is the basic constraint shared by all living beings.

**Social survival coercion**: In the old system, if a woman does not marry, she has no economic source—because women were systematically excluded from the mainstream labor market. If a man does not have children, he has no old-age security—because the old system lacked a comprehensive pension system, relying instead on "raising children for old-age support." In both cases, the individual does not die immediately, but is gradually marginalized by social structures until they can no longer sustain basic life.

The old system was maintained not by "tradition," but by the coercive mechanism of "non-compliance leads to material abandonment."


### 4.2 Economic Independence: Women No Longer Need Marriage to Secure Survival Resources

A core reason the old system could coerce women into marriage was that women were excluded from the mainstream labor market and could not independently obtain survival resources.

This premise is being rewritten.

In 2025, the share of women in higher education reached 50.76%, up from 36.76% in 1995—a 14 percentage point increase (Table 1). China has about 40 million female science and technology workers, accounting for 45.8% of all S&T personnel; globally, women make up 33% of researchers (Table 1). In 2026, Wang Hong won the Fields Medal—the first Chinese citizen to do so and the third woman in history to win it. The national female labor participation rate is about 68%, and women have consistently made up over 43% of total employed persons.

These data point to a single fact: women are gaining economic independence through education and labor participation. When a woman can support herself, marriage ceases to be a "survival necessity" and becomes an "option." This is not a change in ideas, but a change in material conditions.²

> ² Gary Becker, in *A Treatise on the Family* (1981), explained marriage from an economic perspective as a product of division of labor—when women gain economic independence through education and work, the returns to marriage decline. The data presented here can serve as empirical support for that theory.


### 4.3 Old-Age Security: Men No Longer Need Children to Secure Their Later Years

A core reason the old system could coerce men into the "childbearing—breadwinning" track was that the old system lacked universal old-age security; men had to have children to ensure someone would care for them in old age.

This premise is being rewritten.

In 2025, China's population aged 60+ reached 323 million, up 13.07 million from the previous year. The working-age population (16–59) was 851 million, and its share continues to decline (Table 1). Social security and employment expenditures account for about 3.6% of total public budget expenditures. The pension system now covers over 1 billion people.

According to the *China Urban Commercial Elderly Care White Paper (2025)*, more than half of respondents are skeptical of "raising children for old age." 77.7% of middle-aged respondents have already begun to actively prepare for retirement.

When pensions and nursing homes gradually replace "raising children for old-age support," men no longer need children to secure their later years. Having descendants shifts from a "survival task" to a "personal choice." Again, this is not a change in ideas, but a result of institutional substitution.³

> ³ Gøsta Esping-Andersen, in *The Three Worlds of Welfare Capitalism* (1990), introduced the concept of "de-familization," referring to how welfare systems reduce individuals' dependence on the family. China's expanding pension coverage and rising social spending exemplify this process.


### 4.4 Artificial Wombs and Gene Editing: Childbearing No Longer Requires Marriage as a Prerequisite

The most fundamental rule of the old system was that legal childbearing required marriage. This rule bound "childbearing" to "marriage," making them functionally inseparable.

This rule is being challenged by technology.

The "Future of Human Reproduction" project at Lancaster University (2022–2026) systematically assessed three frontier reproductive technologies: ectogenesis (artificial wombs), in vitro gametogenesis, and germline genome editing. Artificial womb technology has advanced in mammalian experiments, enabling simulation of key developmental stages from fertilization to early organ formation.

As these technologies gradually mature and become accessible, childbearing will no longer require sexual intercourse, no longer require a woman's body to undergo pregnancy, and no longer require marriage as a prerequisite. An individual can independently—with their own cells, donor cells, or AI-optimized synthetic genes—go to a fertility center and complete the reproductive process.

Once this rule is dismantled, the last pillar of the old system collapses. Because in the "marriage—childbearing—breadwinning" loop, "legal childbearing requires marriage" is the lock connecting marriage and childbearing. When technology removes that lock, marriage and childbearing become independent, each becoming optional, rather than a bundled necessity.


### 4.5 AI and the Knowledge Economy: Physical Strength Is No Longer Core Productivity

The old gender division of labor—"men earn a living, women keep house"—was built on the material foundation that "physical strength is core productivity."

This foundation is being dissolved by the rise of AI and the knowledge economy. As cognitive ability becomes the primary source of value, women's advantages in reading comprehension, language expression, multitasking, and emotion recognition begin to generate economic value. The rising share of women in global research is itself a manifestation of the release of cognitive capacity.

When women can perform equally or even surpass men in cognitive domains, the old division loses its material base. Men are no longer naturally "breadwinners," and women are no longer naturally "homemakers"—both are becoming individuals who can choose different roles.

This is an early link in a longer chain. When physical strength ceases to be the basis of coercion, the structure of coercion itself begins to loosen. And the loosening of coercion is a precondition for "becoming god"—actively designing one's own mode of existence. Without this loosening, the subsequent discussion cannot proceed.

## 5. Manifestations of the Old System's Disintegration — The Nature of Pressure to Marry/Bear and Baby-Showing


### 5.1 The Old System's Last Gasp

When a system enters the boundary acceleration phase, it does not disappear smoothly. It releases the densest signals before the boundary—because signals must be released before the boundary, otherwise they cannot be released at all. This is not the old system "getting stronger," but "clearing inventory."

Emotional blackmail, moral coercion, social comparison, public opinion pressure—all these signals rising in density simultaneously are essentially the old system's final manifestation before zeroing. The higher the signal density, the shorter the system's remaining life.


### 5.2 Pressuring to Marry and Bear: Attempting to Summon More People Back into the Interval

Those who pressure others to marry and bear children can be divided into two categories.

**Category 1: Those who have executed the old system's tasks.**

They have already completed the "marriage—childbearing—breadwinning" loop and anchored their life's meaning to it. When they see someone refusing to enter this loop, they feel threatened—if someone can live well without this system, then their own choice to enter it was not "the only correct choice" but just "one choice." This cognitive shock is unbearable.

**Category 2: Those who have not yet executed the old system's tasks but believe they must.**

They are enduring the old system's pressure and need a justification to sustain themselves. "Everyone will marry" and "this is the only path in life" are their supporting beliefs. If they see someone refusing to enter, that belief will waver, and they will lose their reason to endure the pressure.

Thus, urging marriage and childbearing is not essentially "caring about you," but "maintaining one's own existential legitimacy." What those people are really saying when they urge you is: "If you can not marry, then what was the meaning of my marriage?"


### 5.3 Baby-Showing: Attempting to Maintain a Signal Circuit at the Boundary

Those who show off their children can also be divided into two categories.

**Category 1: Attempting to use external confirmation to sustain internal beliefs.**

They display their children on social media and collect likes and comments, essentially sending a signal to the old system: "I am still executing this loop, please confirm it is still valid." The number of likes and comments is their attempt to restore signal transmission in a circuit that has already broken down.

**Category 2: Attempting to extend the boundary of their own meaning through their children.**

A child is a causal trace—even if the parents die, the child persists. Showing off one's child is, to some degree, transferring part of one's own meaning to the child, to break through one's own finite interval.

But from the perspective of *The Limit Theory of Meaning*, this operation is structurally invalid: the child's persistence is a causal trace, not the original subject's meaning-bearing signal. Meaning is defined only within the interval in which it is produced; it cannot cross boundaries through traces. The child is an independent system with its own interval; it cannot bear the parents' meaning. Trying to extend one's own meaning through a child is placing the validation button outside one's own interval—a structural error, because the interval where that button lies does not belong to oneself, and one can never receive feedback there.

Pressuring marriage/childbearing and showing off children are two sides of the same behavior: pressuring is "getting others into the interval," showing is "confirming oneself is in the interval." Both are the old system releasing residual signals before the boundary.


### 5.4 The Core Drive Is Fear, Not Love

The old system's operating mechanism is fear, not love.

- Fear of not marrying: loss of survival support, social isolation, no one to care for you in old age.
- Fear of not having children: extinction of the family line, no one to see you off at death.

These fears were real in the old system—because the old system did punish non-compliance. But the effectiveness of fear depends on its premises. When those premises are dismantled one by one by technological, economic, and institutional changes, the power of fear begins to fade. Those who still urge marriage and childbearing still possess fear, but that fear no longer corresponds to reality—it corresponds to the world the old system once constructed, not the world now taking shape.

Mistaking "fear" for "love" is the old system's most persistent self-deception.

## 6. After Release — Four Turns of Meaning


### 6.1 What Is Released Is Not Meaning Itself, but the Anchor of Meaning

After survival coercion disappears, it is not that "meaning disappears," but that the state of "meaning being fixed on a certain track" disappears.

In the old system, meaning was anchored to "completing the path of marriage—childbearing—breadwinning." You did not need to decide what meaning is; the system already decided it for you—you only needed to execute. The advantage of this external anchoring is that you do not need to think. The cost is that you have no choice.

When the anchor is removed, meaning itself does not disappear—it is just no longer fixed on the old track. It must be re-anchored, but the new anchor is no longer external coercion, but internal choice.

This state is often described as "meaning vacuum." This is a misdescription. It is actually an interim period in which "meaning has been released but not yet re-anchored"—meaning has not disappeared; it is transitioning from an external anchor to an internal one.


### 6.2 Turn 1: Task → Closed Loop

**Old model**: Meaning comes from "completing the tasks prescribed by the system." You marry, you bear children, you support the family—you executed the loop, so you are a "qualified person." The validation button is in the old system's hands.

**New model**: Meaning comes from "deciding for yourself what the loop is, and completing it within the interval." You do not need the system to tell you what to do; you need to define for yourself what you want to do—and then do it, completing a full cycle: action → receive feedback → adjust direction. The validation button is not in anyone else's hands, but in your own operation.


### 6.3 Turn 2: External Validation → Internal Verification

**Old model**: Meaning requires others' recognition to confirm. Likes, comments, social approval, peer envy—without these, it is as if you did nothing.

**New model**: Meaning comes from the completeness of the loop itself. You did something, received feedback, adjusted direction—the integrity of this cycle is validation. You do not need others to tell you "you did the right thing," because the completion of the loop itself already contains verification.

This is a corollary of *The Limit Theory of Meaning*: completing a closed-loop operation within the interval provides its own validation, not external confirmation.


### 6.4 Turn 3: Survival Exchange → Free Choice

**Old model**: Relationships were built on survival exchange—men earned a living, women bore children, both exchanged to sustain existence. If one party could not provide the agreed-upon "resources," the relationship would lose its functional foundation.

**New model**: When both sexes can survive independently, relationships are no longer "I need you to live," but "I choose you because I want to live with you." The foundation of relationships is no longer survival necessity, but free choice.

Note: this "choice" is possible precisely because it is built on the basis that "not choosing does not lead to death." Relationships without survival coercion are truly voluntary relationships.


### 6.5 Turn 4: Biological Contingency → Technological Design

**Old model**: Childbearing was a matter of "luck"—children had risks of genetic diseases, childbirth involved pain and life danger, and children's cognitive abilities and health depended on uncontrollable gene combinations. Humans could only accept it, unable to intervene.

**New model**: Artificial wombs and gene editing are transforming childbearing from a "contingent natural process" into a "designable technological operation." Future parents can eliminate major genetic diseases at the embryonic stage and complete the entire pregnancy process in an artificial womb.

This is not "choosing to bear," but "designing to bear." For the first time, humanity has gained the technological capacity to "actively design its own mode of continuation." This is the starting point of becoming god—from passive biological contingency to active design decisions. This turn occurs at the level of childbearing, but its endpoint extends far beyond childbearing itself.

The common structure of these four turns: **from passive acceptance to active construction.** The old system's meaning was given; the new system's meaning is chosen. The old system's relationships were coerced; the new system's relationships are voluntary. The old system's childbearing was contingent; the new system's childbearing is designable.

## 7. After Zeroing — Framework and Boundary Conditions


### 7.1 Boundary Conditions of Meaning

The three core theses of *The Limit Theory of Meaning* are:

1. **Meaning is defined only within the interval.** Beyond the experiential interval (death or system termination), meaning is undefined, not zero.

2. **Meaning must be accomplished through closed-loop operation.** Action → receive feedback → adjust direction—this process must be completed within the interval.

3. **The validation button must be inside the interval.** Placing the validation button outside the interval (posthumous recognition, historical judgment, an afterlife) is a structural error.

Based on these three theses, the old system is falsified: it placed the validation button on the continuity of the old system's rules—after you completed the loop, whether meaning held depended on the system continuing to exist and recognizing you. When the old system disintegrates, those who "completed the old tasks" find that their validation button disappears with the system.

This is not to say "the old system's meaning is false," but rather "the old system's meaning depends on the old system's continued operation"—when the old system disappears, its meaning also loses definition.


### 7.2 Conditions That a New Meaning Framework Must Satisfy

If new meaning must be reconstructed, it should at least satisfy the following conditions:

**Condition 1: Verifiable within the interval.** The new meaning's validation button cannot be placed outside the interval (after death, historical judgment, an afterlife); it must be placed where the individual can verify it during their lifetime.

**Condition 2: Constructed autonomously by the individual.** The old system's meaning was externally given; the new system's meaning must be determined by the individual themselves. This does not mean "anything goes"—it means the individual must define their own loop, rather than accepting a pre-set loop from the system.

**Condition 3: Forming a closed-loop operation.** A single action is insufficient to constitute meaning; it requires a complete cycle: action → receive feedback → adjust direction. The integrity of meaning comes from the completeness of the loop, not from the action itself.

These three conditions do not provide the content of meaning, only its formal constraints.


### 7.3 "Common Survival Entitlement + Exclusive Private Property Rights" as an Institutional Response

Meaning needs a stable space in which to be constructed. If the old system's collapse plunges individuals into survival instability, then the release of meaning will be interrupted by a "re-coercion of survival"—individuals will be forced again to focus on "how to survive" rather than "how to construct meaning." Therefore, the institutional level needs to provide a basic condition—not meaning itself, but the spatial conditions in which meaning can be constructed.

This space needs to satisfy two directions:

1. **Common survival entitlement**: ensuring that no individual is abandoned by social structures, regardless of whether they marry, bear children, or follow traditional occupations. This is the foundation of the individual's space after the release of meaning—if one still needs to "execute old system tasks" to obtain survival resources, then the old system's coercion still exists.

2. **Exclusive private property rights**: preserving individual space for creative accumulation, allowing individuals to build their own closed loops of meaning through their own actions. If all property is communally owned, individuals lose the drive to actively construct meaning.

Together, these two constitute a framework: **no one is abandoned, but everyone has the freedom to construct their own loop.** This framework is not "new meaning" itself, but the spatial condition in which new meaning can be constructed.


### 7.4 Rudiments of a New Reproductive Model

**Decoupling of childbearing and marriage**: Childbearing will become a technological operation independent of marriage. Marriage will revert to its original function—two people choosing to live together, no longer bearing the social function of "legal reproduction."

**Dissolution of gender roles**: When "men earn a living, women bear children" no longer holds, gender loses its function as a basis for role allocation. Gender will retreat to being "one dimension of individual difference."

**Reconstruction of intergenerational relations**: From "you are born of me, so you must obey me" to "I choose to have you, so I am willing to accompany your growth."

**Lag of the education system**: The education system still trains students to enter a disappearing old world, leading to a "version mismatch" for an entire generation—they are trained to adapt to the old system, yet thrown into the new world.

## 8. Conclusion — Why This Is All Happening

The preceding sections have described the old system's disintegration, the occurrence of boundary acceleration, the release of coercion, and the turn of meaning. "Becoming god" has been mentioned at three points: the introduction identified it as the endpoint of this paper; section 4.5 identified the loosening of coercion as its precondition; section 6.5 identified technological design as its starting point. It can now be formally discussed.


### 8.1 The Causal Chain

Old system coercion → technological dismantling → boundary acceleration → release of coercion → meaning vacuum → four turns → threshold of becoming god. This is not a matter of individual choice, but systemic necessity.


### 8.2 The Old System Is Returning to Zero

The four pillars of the old system—women's economic dependence on marriage, men's dependence on childbearing for old-age security, physical strength as core productivity, and childbearing as the sole legal product of marriage—are being dismantled by technology. The data already reflect this: marriage rates are falling, fertility rates are falling, the working-age population is shrinking, and concepts of old-age support are shifting.

The historically peak intensity of pressure to marry and bear children is not the old system getting stronger, but it releasing residual signals before the boundary. Before zeroing, there must be a final manifestation.


### 8.3 Meaning Is Turning

After the old system's coercion disappears, meaning does not automatically appear, but it does not disappear either. It merely shifts from "externally given task" to "internally constructed choice." The four directions of turn are already clear: from task to closed-loop, from external validation to internal verification, from survival exchange to free choice, from biological contingency to technological design. Meaning is being released from the old system's track and re-anchored onto individual choice.


### 8.4 The Threshold of Becoming God Is Visible

"Becoming god," as used in this paper, refers to humanity's transition from "being governed by natural laws" to "actively designing its own mode of existence." This process has three concrete dimensions:

- The right to choose one's own mode of continuation—childbearing transforms from contingency to design.
- The right to determine one's own source of meaning—from external tasks to internal closed loops.
- The takeover of one's own social role definition—from mandatory options to optional ones.

These three layers of capacity together constitute the first condition for humanity, at the species level, to actively design its own mode of existence.


### 8.5 The Reproductive Revolution Is a Threshold, Not an Endpoint

The significance of the reproductive revolution is not that "humans can have children without marriage." Its significance is that it is the first time humanity has exercised the power of "design" at the level of "species continuation." "Species continuation" is the last constraint that the old system placed on humanity. Humanity has transformed the environment, transformed society, transformed institutions, but has never transformed the process by which it is itself produced. The reproductive revolution is opening that door.

Once that door opens, the old system's coercive force will be invalidated. Any argument that attempts to constrain human reproductive choices by invoking "natural law" or "tradition" will lose its coercive foundation. Humanity will transition from a "species governed by natural laws" to a "being that actively designs its own mode of existence." This is not the end. It is the first threshold.


### 8.6 Falsifiability Conditions

The core judgment of this paper—"the old system is approaching its boundary, and its coercion is being released"—is testable. If the following conditions are observed within 20 years, this paper's conclusions will be falsified:

1. **Marriage and fertility rates continuously rise back above replacement level.** If the old system were truly "zeroing out," its execution rate would not rebound. If more people re-enter the old system, it would indicate that coercion remains effective.

2. **"Not marrying" and "not bearing children" once again become survival risks that cannot be absorbed by social structures.** If social institutions regress so that women must obtain survival resources through marriage and men must secure old-age support through childbearing, it would indicate that the old system's coercion has not been dismantled, only temporarily weakened.

3. **Technological alternatives are systematically prohibited or marginalized.** If artificial wombs, gene editing, and similar technologies are globally banned, unable to serve as optional reproductive paths, it would indicate that the old system has reconsolidated its boundaries through political means.

If any of these conditions holds, this paper's description is wrong. If they are not observed, this paper's framework continues to hold.


### 8.7 Closing

The old system is returning to zero. Survival coercion is being released. The anchor of meaning is being untied from the old system's track.

What can be said has already been said here. The rest is left to silence. Wittgenstein said the same at the end of the *Tractatus*—not because we have nothing to say, but because what lies beyond the boundary is simply not within the domain of the sayable.
