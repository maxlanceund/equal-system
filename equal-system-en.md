## Abstract

This paper proposes a new social framework called the "Equal System." Its core structure is: **shared subsistence rights + exclusive private property rights**.

The system is designed to address two fundamental problems:
1. Remove survival compulsion, so that individuals are no longer locked into a single direction by subsistence needs.
2. Retain private property as a driver for transcendent pursuits.

This paper presents the complete logical derivation from its philosophical foundation, and elaborates on its institutional structure, technological prerequisites, and transition pathways.


## I. Logical Derivation: From the Limit of Meaning to the Equal System

### 1.1 Three Verifiable Premises

**Premise 1: Observer Finitude**

The human individual is a finite system with a beginning, an end, and an operational time window. Meaning (including sense of direction, value, and purpose) depends on this system's ability to receive and process signals. When the system ceases to operate, meaning loses its carrier.

**Premise 2: Condition Dependence**

Meaning is not a fixed attribute, but a signal that changes with system states, external conditions, and time. When external conditions change, the structure and direction of meaning also change.

**Premise 3: Verifiability Exclusion**

Any claim that depends on a commitment "beyond the interval" (such as "history will repay," "there is a place after death," or "the future will inevitably arrive") belongs to the category of the unverifiable and should not be included in an operable framework.

### 1.3 Derivation Steps

**Step 1: Survival Compulsion is the Reason Meaning is Locked**

In existing systems, individuals are forced to participate in labor to maintain survival — regardless of whether that labor aligns with their direction or will. This "survival compulsion" is the basic mechanism by which the system locks individuals into a single direction.

**Derivation**: If survival compulsion is the reason meaning is locked, then eliminating survival compulsion will release the direction of meaning.

**Step 2: Technology is Eliminating Subsistence Scarcity**

AI automation, nuclear fusion energy, space resource extraction, and other technologies are driving the production cost of subsistence goods toward zero. If subsistence goods can be automatically supplied by the system without relying on individual labor, then survival compulsion can be removed.

**Derivation**: If the cost of producing subsistence goods approaches zero, then subsistence rights can be automatically supplied by the system without needing to be obtained through labor.

**Step 3: Subsistence Guarantee Releases Directional Signals**

When survival no longer needs to be exchanged for labor, the individual's directional signal shifts from "maintaining survival" to "pursuing transcendence" — creation, exploration, connection, life extension.

**Derivation**: If subsistence is guaranteed, directional signals will automatically shift toward transcendent goals.

**Step 4: Private Property Function Transfers**

In existing systems, the function of private property is "acquiring subsistence goods." When subsistence goods no longer need to be acquired, the function of private property will automatically transfer to "acquiring credentials for transcendence."

**Derivation**: If the function of private property shifts from "survival exchange" to "credentials for transcendence," then private property is no longer an obstacle to survival but a driver of transcendence.

**Step 5: A Dual-Track Parallel Structure Naturally Emerges**

When the subsistence layer is automatically supplied by the system and the transcendent layer is driven by private property, a dual-track parallel structure of "shared subsistence + exclusive private property" naturally forms.

**Derivation**: If the functions of the subsistence layer and the private layer are clearly separated, they can operate in parallel within the same system, without interfering with or replacing each other.


## II. Core Principles

The Equal System is built on three core principles:

1. **Subsistence rights are non-tradable**: Access to food, water, housing, basic healthcare, and basic education should not depend on an individual's labor capacity or market transactions. They should be provided to all individuals as the system's default state.

2. **Private property rights are inviolable**: All goods, assets, and resources beyond the subsistence level remain within the sphere of private property. Individuals can acquire and accumulate private property through labor, creation, trade, inheritance, and other means.

3. **Subsistence and transcendence are separated**: Subsistence is handled by the shared layer; transcendence is handled by the private layer. The two layers operate in parallel without interference.

## IV. Technological Foundations

The feasibility of the Equal System is based on the following technological trends:

1. **AI-Automated Production**: When the production of basic goods can be automated by AI systems, "labor" is no longer a prerequisite for survival.
2. **Controlled Nuclear Fusion**: Unlimited energy makes the production cost of basic goods approach zero.
3. **Space Resource Extraction**: The scale of resources in the solar system and beyond far exceeds that of Earth, fundamentally changing the definition of "scarcity."
4. **Longevity and Life Sciences**: When subsistence is guaranteed, directional signals naturally turn toward the pursuit of "extending the interval."

## VI. Responses to Common Objections

**1. Won't shared subsistence make people lazy?**

No. When survival is no longer a forced issue, directional signals naturally shift toward creation, exploration, and connection. The driving force does not come from "lack" but from "direction."

**2. Won't private property lead to new inequalities?**

At the transcendent level, differences are reasonable and necessary. The Equal System does not eliminate all differences — it only eliminates those caused by "survival compulsion."

**3. Is this system a utopia?**

No. It is not based on "ideal human nature" but on "technological feasibility." If the technological conditions are met, the system will naturally adjust.


## VII. Conclusion

The Equal System is not an idealistic "should" — it is a structural observation of how systems will naturally adjust when technological conditions change.

It describes the transition path of human civilization from "laboring to survive" to "living to create."

It does not promise to eliminate all problems, but it promises to eliminate the most fundamental one: **survival compulsion**.

When survival is no longer something that must be obtained through labor, the directional signal of human civilization will automatically turn toward farther horizons: space exploration, life extension, consciousness transcendence.


## Note

This design is derived from the "Limit Theory of Meaning" framework and has not yet been validated through large-scale social experimentation. Its feasibility depends on the gradual maturation of technological conditions such as controlled nuclear fusion, AI automation, and space resource extraction.

Its value lies not in "already being realized," but in "being available as an actionable option in the foreseeable future."
