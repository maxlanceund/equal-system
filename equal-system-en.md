## Abstract

This paper proposes a new social framework called the "Equal System." Its core structure is: **shared subsistence rights + exclusive private property rights**.

The system is designed to address two fundamental problems:
1. Remove survival compulsion, so that individuals are no longer locked into a single direction by subsistence needs.
2. Retain private property as a driver for transcendent pursuits.

This paper presents the complete logical derivation from its philosophical foundation, and elaborates on its institutional structure, technological prerequisites, and transition pathways.


## I. Logical Derivation: From the Limit of Meaning to the Equal System

### 1.1 Three Verifiable Premises

**Premise 1: Observer Finitude**

The human individual is a finite system with a beginning, an end, and an operational time window. Meaning (including sense of direction, value, and purpose) depends on this system's ability to receive and process signals. When the system ceases to operate, meaning loses its carrier.

**Premise 2: Condition Dependence**

Meaning is not a fixed attribute, but a signal that changes with system states, external conditions, and time. When external conditions change, the structure and direction of meaning also change.

**Premise 3: Verifiability Exclusion**

Any claim that depends on a commitment "beyond the interval" (such as "history will repay," "there is a place after death," or "the future will inevitably arrive") belongs to the category of the unverifiable and should not be included in an operable framework.
