# Equal System: A Social Structure Design for the Post-Scarcity Era

> **Core Thesis**: This paper proposes a new social framework—the Equal System—whose core structure is "shared subsistence rights + exclusive private property rights." The system is designed to remove survival compulsion, so that individuals are no longer locked into a single direction by subsistence needs, while retaining private property as a driver for transcendent pursuits. It is not an idealistic "ought" proposal, but a structural observation of how institutions will naturally adjust as technological conditions change.


## Abstract

This paper proposes a new social framework called the "Equal System." Its core structure is: **shared subsistence rights + exclusive private property rights**.

The system is designed to address two fundamental problems:
1. Remove survival compulsion, so that individuals are no longer locked into a single direction by subsistence needs.
2. Retain private property as a driver for transcendent pursuits.

This paper presents the complete logical derivation from its philosophical foundation, and elaborates on its institutional structure, technological prerequisites, and transition pathways.


## I. Logical Derivation: From the Limit of Meaning to the Equal System

### 1.1 Three Verifiable Premises

**Premise 1: Observer Finitude**

The human individual is a finite system with a beginning, an end, and an operational time window. Meaning (including sense of direction, value, and purpose) depends on this system's ability to receive and process signals. When the system ceases to operate, meaning loses its carrier.

**Premise 2: Condition Dependence**

Meaning is not a fixed attribute, but a signal that changes with system states, external conditions, and time. When external conditions change, the structure and direction of meaning also change.

**Premise 3: Verifiability Exclusion**

Any claim that depends on a commitment "beyond the interval" (such as "history will repay," "there is a place after death," or "the future will inevitably arrive") belongs to the category of the unverifiable and should not be included in an operable framework.

### 1.3 Derivation Steps

**Step 1: Survival compulsion is the reason meaning is locked**

In existing systems, individuals are forced to participate in labor to maintain survival—regardless of whether the labor aligns with their direction or will. This "survival compulsion" is the basic mechanism by which the system locks individuals into a single direction.

**Derivation**: If survival compulsion is the reason meaning is locked, then eliminating survival compulsion will release the direction of meaning.

**Step 2: Technology is eliminating subsistence scarcity**

AI automation, nuclear fusion, and space resource extraction are driving the production cost of subsistence goods toward zero. If subsistence goods can be automatically supplied by the system without relying on individual labor, then survival compulsion can be removed.

**Derivation**: If the production cost of subsistence goods approaches zero, then subsistence rights can be automatically supplied by the system without requiring labor in exchange.

**Step 3: Subsistence guarantee releases directional signals**

When subsistence no longer needs to be earned through labor, the individual's directional signal shifts from "maintaining survival" to "pursuing transcendence"—creation, exploration, connection, and life extension.

**Derivation**: If subsistence is guaranteed, directional signals will automatically turn toward transcendent goals.

**Step 4: Private property's function shifts**

In existing systems, private property functions as a means of "acquiring subsistence goods." When subsistence goods no longer need to be acquired, private property's function will automatically shift to "qualification for transcendent pursuits."

**Derivation**: If private property's function shifts from "survival exchange" to "transcendence credentials," then private property ceases to be an obstacle to survival and becomes a driver for transcendence.

**Step 5: The dual-track structure naturally forms**

When the subsistence layer is automatically supplied by the system and the transcendence layer is driven by private property, a dual-track structure naturally emerges: "shared subsistence + exclusive private property."

**Derivation**: If the subsistence layer and private layer are clearly separated in function, they can run in parallel within the same system, without interfering with or replacing each other.


## II. Core Principles

The Equal System is built on three core principles:

1. **Subsistence rights are non-tradable**: Access to food, water, housing, basic healthcare, and basic education should not depend on individual labor capacity or market transactions. They should be provided as the system's default state to all individuals.

2. **Private property rights are inviolable**: All goods, assets, and resources beyond the subsistence level remain within the sphere of private property. Individuals can acquire and accumulate private property through labor, creation, trade, inheritance, and other means.

3. **Separation of subsistence and transcendence**: Subsistence issues are handled by the shared layer; transcendence issues are handled by the private layer. The two layers operate in parallel without interference.

| Driving Mechanism | Profit incentives, creativity release, exploratory desire |
| Functional Positioning | Serves as credentials for "transcendence qualification," not as a tool for "survival exchange" |


## IV. Technological Foundations

The feasibility of the Equal System rests on the following technological trends:

1. **AI automated production**: When the production of basic goods can be fully automated by AI systems, "labor" is no longer a prerequisite for survival.
2. **Controlled nuclear fusion**: Unlimited energy pushes the production cost of basic goods toward zero.
3. **Space resource extraction**: The resource scale of the solar system—and the galaxy—far exceeds that of Earth, radically redefining "scarcity."
4. **Longevity and life sciences**: Once subsistence is guaranteed, directional signals naturally turn toward the pursuit of "extending the interval."

## VI. Responses to Common Objections

**1. Would shared subsistence make people lazy?**

No. When survival is no longer a forced issue, directional signals naturally turn toward creation, exploration, and connection. The driving force comes not from "deprivation" but from "direction."

**2. Would private property lead to new inequalities?**

At the transcendent level, differences are both reasonable and necessary. The Equal System does not eliminate all differences—it only eliminates the differences caused by "survival compulsion."

**3. Is this system a utopia?**

No. It is not based on "ideal human nature," but on "technological feasibility." If the technological conditions are met, institutions will naturally adjust accordingly.


## VII. Conclusion

The Equal System is not an idealistic "ought" proposal, but a structural observation of how institutions will naturally adjust as technological conditions change.

It describes the transitional pathway of humanity from "laboring to survive" to "surviving to create."

It does not promise to eliminate all problems, but it does promise to eliminate the most fundamental one: **survival compulsion**.

When subsistence is no longer something that must be earned through labor, the directional signal of human civilization will automatically turn toward farther horizons: space exploration, life extension, and the transcendence of consciousness.


## Note

This design is derived from the "Limit Theory of Meaning" framework and has not yet been validated through large-scale social experimentation. Its feasibility depends on the gradual maturation of technological conditions such as controlled nuclear fusion, AI automation, and space resource extraction.

Its value lies not in "having been realized," but in being "considerable as an operable option in the foreseeable future."
